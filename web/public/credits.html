Unofficial self-hosted Counter-Strike 1.6 webgame. Counter-Strike, Half-Life, maps, models and sounds belong to Valve. Assets are read from this host’s installed Steam copy. Not affiliated with Valve.

Browser engine: Xash3D FWGS (GPL-3.0), CS16Client (GPL-2.0+), ReGameDLL_CS (GPL-3.0), YaPB (MIT), webxash3d-fwgs wrapper, fflate (MIT), node-datachannel (MPL-2.0), ws (MIT).

The browser bundle and WebRTC bridge are derived from CSweb and sla-cs16-web, MIT licensed. Original notices and build sources are included with this installation. Community branding and unrelated features are excluded.

Game servers listen only inside the container network. Guests connect through this web host’s WebRTC bridge. Invite rooms are unlisted; anyone with the room link can join.
